Intent

Cognition since 1.0 aow:Intent

A structured, actionable goal derived from an Objective and interpretable by agents, orchestrators, and people.

States a business goal in a form that can be planned, assigned, governed, and verified.

Attributes

AttributeTypeRequiredDescription
goal_statementtextyesWhat the Intent is meant to achieve, stated as an outcome rather than a procedure.
constraintsstring-listFree-text constraints that are not (yet) captured as Policies.
priorityone of low, medium, high, criticalRelative priority.
acceptable_risk_bandone of low, medium, highThe level of residual risk the requester accepts.
statusone of proposed, active, fulfilled, abandonedWhere the Intent is in its life.

Every entity also has an id and a type, and may carry label, description, and the provenance facet: version, created_at, created_by, revision_of.

Example

Source: examples/claims-processing.jsonld. The example passes all validation checks.

{
  "id": "intent-eligibility-2026",
  "type": "Intent",
  "label": "Automate eligibility determination",
  "goal_statement": "Determine eligibility for incoming disability claims automatically wherever policy allows, and route the rest to an adjuster with the work already done.",
  "serves_objective": "obj-claims-cycle-time",
  "shaped_by": [
    "ctx-claims-procedure"
  ],
  "constrained_by": [
    "policy-auto-approval",
    "policy-high-risk-review"
  ],
  "requires_assurance": "aow:AL2",
  "acceptable_risk_band": "low",
  "priority": "high",
  "status": "active"
}
