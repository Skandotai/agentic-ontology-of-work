Ontology / Perception

Work Item

Perception new in 2.0 since 2.0 aow:WorkItem

The unit of business work that the rest of the ontology is about: a claim, an application, an order, a case, a ticket.

Identifies the item that Intents, Tasks, Actions, and Observations refer to, so work on one item can be followed across systems, agents, and people.

Attributes

AttributeTypeRequiredDescription
item_typestringyesThe kind of work item, for example "insurance-claim" or "purchase-order".
external_refstringIdentifier of the item in its system of record.
statestringCurrent business state of the item, in the vocabulary of its system of record.
opened_atdatetimeWhen the item entered the process.
closed_atdatetimeWhen the item left the process, if it has.

Every entity also has an id and a type, and may carry label, description, and the provenance facet: version, created_at, created_by, revision_of.

Notes

In version 1.0 the work item was implicit in Context (for example, "ctx-claim-84933"). As a separate entity, it can be referenced by several Intents and Tasks.

Corresponds to a case or object in process mining (IEEE 1849 XES and the OCEL 2.0 object-centric event log format).
