Ontology / Cognition

Plan

Cognition new in 2.0 since 2.0 aow:Plan

An ordered or partially ordered set of Tasks intended to fulfill an Intent, produced by an Orchestrator or other Actor.

Separates planning from execution, so plans can be reviewed, approved, and compared.

Attributes

AttributeTypeRequiredDescription
statusone of draft, approved, executing, completed, failed, abandonedWhere the Plan is in its life.
exception_protocoltextWhat to do when a Task fails or a Policy blocks progress.

Every entity also has an id and a type, and may carry label, description, and the provenance facet: version, created_at, created_by, revision_of.

Notes

Listed as an optional concept in version 1.0; a full entity in 2.0.

The Orchestrator's dependency_graph in version 1.0 is the Plan's set of Tasks and their depends_on links.
