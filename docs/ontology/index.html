Classes

aow:WorkItem Work Item Perception

The unit of business work that the rest of the ontology is about: a claim, an application, an order, a case, a ticket.

Full definition →

aow:Signal Signal Perception

A raw, time-stamped trace emitted by a system, a person, or the environment: an event, a log line, a user-interface interaction, a document arriving, a state change, a metric sample.

Full definition →

aow:Observation Observation Perception

An interpreted fact about a Work Item, Actor, or Action, derived from one or more Signals and carrying a Confidence.

Full definition →

aow:Objective Objective Cognition

A business-level goal expressed in strategic or operational terms.

Full definition →

aow:Intent Intent Cognition

A structured, actionable goal derived from an Objective and interpretable by agents, orchestrators, and people.

Full definition →

aow:Context Context Cognition

The structured situational information that gives relevance to an Intent.

Full definition →

aow:Policy Policy Cognition

Declarative constraints defining permissible, required, or restricted behavior.

Full definition →

aow:Plan Plan Cognition

An ordered or partially ordered set of Tasks intended to fulfill an Intent, produced by an Orchestrator or other Actor.

Full definition →

aow:Task Task Execution

A unit of assigned work within a Plan, performed by an Actor filling a Role.

Full definition →

aow:Actor Actor Execution

Anything that can be assigned Tasks, perform Actions, and be held to account for them: an Agent, a person, an Orchestrator, or a Guardian.

Full definition →

aow:Agent Agent Execution

A software Actor that interprets Intents and Tasks and acts on them using Skills, within the autonomy it has been cleared for. A kind of Actor.

Full definition →

aow:HumanActor Human Actor Execution

A person who performs, approves, reviews, or oversees work. A kind of Actor.

Full definition →

aow:Orchestrator Orchestrator Execution

A coordinating Actor that turns Intents into Plans, assigns Tasks to Actors, and manages sequencing and exceptions. A kind of Actor.

Full definition →

aow:Guardian Guardian Assurance

The oversight Actor that enforces Policy and Assurance Levels, monitors behavior, and escalates to people when needed. A kind of Actor.

Full definition →

aow:Role Role Execution

A named set of responsibilities and authority that an Actor can fill, such as "claims adjuster" or "eligibility reviewer".

Full definition →

aow:Skill Skill Execution

A reusable capability that an Actor can invoke.

Full definition →

aow:Action Action Execution

A single execution event in which an Actor invokes a Skill, in service of a Task, at a point in time.

Full definition →

aow:Result Result Execution

The immediate, atomic effect or output of an Action.

Full definition →

aow:Confidence Confidence Assurance

A model-, rule-, or human-derived measure of certainty about an Observation, a Result, or an Action.

Full definition →

aow:AssuranceLevel Assurance Level Assurance

A governance-set level of permitted autonomy, with the human oversight and evidence it requires.

Full definition →

aow:Outcome Outcome Assurance

The aggregated business impact of one or more Results, measured against an Objective.

Full definition →

aow:Feedback Feedback Assurance

Structured information, from people or systems, used to improve future behavior.

Full definition →

aow:Memory Memory Assurance

Persistent knowledge accumulated from experience, used to inform future Context.

Full definition →

aow:OperationalMemory Operational Memory Assurance

Short-lived, execution-oriented memory scoped to a session, Task, or Work Item. A kind of Memory.

Full definition →

aow:KnowledgeBase Knowledge Base Assurance

Long-lived memory of generalized patterns, rules, and precedents. A kind of Memory.

Assurance Levels

aow:AL0 Manual

People: Performs the work. Agents: Observes and prepares; takes no Action that changes a Work Item.

Review: Not applicable; the work is done by people.

aow:AL1 Assisted

People: Decides. Approves every Action that changes a Work Item before it takes effect. Agents: Recommends, drafts, and prepares.

Review: Every state-changing Action carries approved_by.

aow:AL2 Delegated

People: Confirms when confidence is low or a Policy triggers; reviews a sample otherwise. Agents: Decides and acts within Policy.

Review: Escalate below the confidence threshold or on Policy trigger; sampled review otherwise.

aow:AL3 Supervised

People: Reviews by exception, when the Guardian raises an alert, and in periodic audit. Agents: Acts, and must be able to reverse or compensate its Actions.

Review: Guardian-raised exceptions and periodic audit; Skills used must be reversible.

aow:AL4 Autonomous

People: Sets Policy and monitors aggregate Outcomes. Agents: Acts without routine human review; the Guardian can suspend it at any time.

Review: Continuous Guardian monitoring; audit of aggregate Outcomes.
